Lexicon — Load-Bearing Boundary (Second Generation)

Language in a system is load-bearing.

Terms shape reasoning, constrain interpretation, and influence behavior. Drift in language produces drift in systems.

For this reason, a lexicon is not defined in advance.

Definitions are written only after terms have demonstrated stability through repeated, real use. Concepts must prove that they carry consistent meaning before they are named, formalized, or canonized.

Authorship precedes definition.
Use precedes canonization.

This repository does not maintain a comprehensive lexicon. Instead, terms are allowed to emerge organically and are defined only when their meaning becomes unavoidable.

Premature definition is avoided to prevent rigidity, false precision, and semantic overreach.
