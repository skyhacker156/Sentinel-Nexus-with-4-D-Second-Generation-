What This File Actually Is (core truth)

This file is not a product spec, a philosophy essay, or an ethical manifesto.

At its core, it captures one boundary decision Patrick made:

gosu chess is a personal decision-navigation tool, not a system intended for others until validated through direct use.

Everything else exists to justify that boundary.

Personal vs. universal distinction
Some tools are built for individual navigation, not general adoption.

Validation precedes sharing
A tool should not guide others until it has been used extensively by its creator.

Freedom to revise requires privacy
Keeping a tool private allows iteration without obligation or reputational lock-in.

Separation of tools and truths
Personal decision aids are not the same as universal frameworks.
