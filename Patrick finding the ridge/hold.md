At its core, this file documents the moment Patrick discovered a repeatable decision-making structure and recognized it as a ridge.

Stripped of praise, projections, and future plans, the essence is:

Complex decisions can be decomposed into a bounded set of strategies, evaluated comparatively, executed stepwise, and logged to produce reusable patterns.
Decision-making as infrastructure
Decisions are treated as a system, not intuition.

Multi-option comparison
Generating multiple strategies before choosing reduces bias.

Explicit evaluation before action
Decisions are compared before execution, not justified after.

Stepwise execution with wait states
Execution is broken into manageable moves, with pauses when information is missing.

Provenance & pattern extraction
Decisions are logged so future decisions can reuse prior structures.
