Despite its length and OS-kernel framing, this document is about one thing:

A structured environment for reasoning where uncertainty is transformed into action-ready clarity through staged progression and explicit closure.

Everything else is scaffolding to explain that idea.
Reasoning as a bounded environment
Uncertainty is not handled ad hoc; it is entered deliberately.

Explicit causal origin
Each reasoning sequence begins with a clearly defined question.

Staged progression with pauses
Reasoning unfolds through moves and waitstates, not continuous chatter.

Explicit closure
Uncertainty is collapsed deliberately into an action-ready artifact.

Self-refinement through history
Past decisions inform future ones without external training or optimization.

That’s the Arena.
